# IPF manuscript evidence-chain audit

## Completed in this revision

- Re-ran the core analyses from the archived GEO-derived input files for GSE22011, GSE150910, GSE134692, GSE124685, and GSE135893.
- Performed an exploratory ComBat sensitivity analysis on 31 signature genes common to GSE150910, donor-merged GSE134692, and GSE124685.
- Preserved IPF, CHP, and ALI indicators in the ComBat design; summarized GSE124685 at patient level after correction.
- The direction of all four predefined case–control contrasts was preserved, but effect magnitudes changed. This is reported as sensitivity evidence, not independent validation.
- Archived corrected matrix, score comparison, sample manifest, GSE134692 library-to-donor manifest, GSE124685 sample manifest, software versions, and input SHA-256 hashes.
- Removed GSE47460 pulmonary-function results from the present-study evidence chain because its expression matrix was not independently reanalyzed.
- Revised the title, abstract, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, data-availability statement, and principal-results table accordingly.

## Interpretation boundary

The manuscript now supports partial and context-dependent transferability of an in-vitro stiffness-associated program. It does not establish that the score measures human tissue stiffness, predicts IPF severity or prognosis, or has causal or biomarker validity.
